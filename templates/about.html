What is IsoSphere?

v1.0 Stable
The Core Idea
When People Hear "Nuclear," They Think Power Plants.
IsoSphere Shows You Everything Else.
Nuclear science is everywhere — in the hospital where you get a PET scan, in the food supply chain that keeps imports safe, in the factory that checks welds on an oil pipeline, in the satellite exploring deep space. But most people — including engineers, policymakers, and students — have no single place to explore what these technologies actually do, how much they cost, or how they compare to conventional alternatives. IsoSphere is that place. It is an interactive intelligence platform that lets you simulate nuclear technology deployments across 12 sectors, compare them against traditional methods with real data, and ask an AI to explain the results in plain language.
Understand
Nuclear technology is surrounded by misconception and information asymmetry. IsoSphere cuts through the noise by presenting factual, data-driven profiles for each sector — what the technology does, where it is used in the real world, and what its measurable outcomes are. No hype, no fear — just numbers and context.
Compare
Is nuclear actually better than the alternative? That depends on the sector, the scale, and what you are optimizing for. IsoSphere's Comparison Engine puts nuclear and traditional technologies side-by-side across six metrics — cost, efficiency, accuracy, ROI, safety, and environmental impact — so you can see exactly where each approach wins and loses.
Decide
Data alone is not enough — you need interpretation. IsoSphere's AI Advisor takes your simulation or comparison results and explains them in natural language: why one technology outperformed another, what the trade-offs mean in practice, and what a decision-maker should consider before committing resources.

The Awareness Gap
Most people — including engineers, students, and policymakers — associate nuclear science exclusively with power generation. In reality, isotope technologies are used in over 100 countries for medical diagnosis, food safety, water management, industrial quality control, and environmental protection. This gap between what nuclear science actually does and what people think it does leads to missed opportunities, unfounded resistance, and poor technology choices. IsoSphere exists to close that gap with data, not rhetoric.
Privacy-First Design
IsoSphere's AI Advisor runs entirely on your local machine using Ollama — no queries are sent to external servers, no data leaves your device, and no usage is tracked. Your simulation results, comparisons, and questions stay private. The platform is designed for environments where confidentiality matters: government agencies, research institutions, and corporate strategy teams evaluating nuclear technology options.
